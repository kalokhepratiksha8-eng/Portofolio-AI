Process

Three Simple Steps

From zero to a full career readiness report in under 2 minutes.

1

Add Your Links

Add a GitHub username, LinkedIn or portfolio URL, paste resume text, or upload a text-based PDF. Any one signal works; scanned PDFs may not extract.

2

Review your signals

The backend reads public GitHub signals, checks resume/PDF text, analyzes pasted LinkedIn text, and reviews bounded public portfolio HTML. LinkedIn URLs alone are format-checked.

3

Get Your Report

Get module scores and practical suggestions. Job-match cards elsewhere on this page are examples, not calculated results.
